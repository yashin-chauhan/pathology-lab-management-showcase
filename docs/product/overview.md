# 📋 Product Overview: Medilab Pathology Management

## 🎯 Vision & Purpose
**Medilab** is an end-to-end diagnostic healthcare and pathology laboratory management system designed to eliminate manual appointment paper-slips, reduce patient clinic waiting times, streamline online test fee collections, and securely deliver digital pathology reports to patients 24/7.

---

## 🛑 Problem Statement

Diagnostic pathology clinics face recurring operational friction points:
1. **Unorganized Test Catalogs**: Patients frequently call or visit clinics simply to inquire about test availability, sample requirements (e.g. 12-hour fasting), and pricing.
2. **Manual Appointment Queues**: Walk-in registration creates congestion at reception desks, leading to lost time and administrative errors.
3. **Delayed Test Report Collection**: Patients must make physical round-trips to the laboratory to collect paper printouts of blood and pathology test results.
4. **Payment Tracking Difficulties**: Handling cash at counters creates reconciliation overhead and billing disputes.

---

## 💡 The Solution

Medilab introduces a digitized, patient-centric workflow:
- **Instant A-Z Test Search**: Patients quickly look up tests alphabetically, review pricing and preparation guidelines.
- **Online Booking & Razorpay Payment**: Instant scheduling with chosen doctors or pathology departments paired with online payments.
- **Direct Digital Report Delivery**: Lab technicians upload reports once tests are completed, automatically making them accessible on the patient's authenticated dashboard.
- **Centralized Admin CMS**: Streamlined management of test offerings, doctor rosters, patient records, and appointment schedules.
